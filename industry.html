Professional Summary

I'm a First Class Honours graduate in Mathematical Sciences and Statistics from the Australian National University, specialising in algebraic topology. I started uni at 15, and finished the double degree at 20.

These days I deliver analytics and Power BI decision support solutions: leading project delivery end to end, presenting findings to SES Band 1 and 2 executives, and translating complex quantitative outputs into practical business decisions. Before that I spent three years governing enterprise-scale data and reporting in the Australian Public Service, and I've been teaching and tutoring mathematics for seven years.

I combine rigorous quantitative training with auditable reporting pipelines, workflow automation, and stakeholder engagement across all levels. I work best in fast-paced environments where rigorous thinking meets real-world outcomes.
